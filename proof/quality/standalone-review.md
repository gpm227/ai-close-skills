# Using each skill on its own

We reviewed the 37 individual downloads for a first-time user who brings their own work. For each job we checked the starting material, the decision or deliverable, and what happens when an input is missing. Each skill now has a request you can copy into ChatGPT with your own material.

Twenty-seven workflows needed a substantive repair. Ten kept their existing workflow after inspection of a usable result. The table records the deciding example for each. Fresh operators used fictional business inputs and only the relevant instructions for 24 jobs across sales, outbound, marketing, brand and operations. The three reporting workflows and the directory exporter also received targeted arithmetic, source-row or executable checks. Previous independent trials supplied comparison cases and evidence for retained workflows. The work here tests instructions and artifacts. A live workspace installation, a customer's use of a result and its business impact require separate observation.
