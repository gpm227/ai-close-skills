**Proposed proof copy and composition.** Lead with “Keep the shelf run readable.” Make the focal point a labeled wireframe with the same two rows, **first call number** and **last call number**, carried from **01 · Before packing** to **02 · After shelving**. The crew writes both on the route sheet and compares them with the reshelved run. Connect each recorded endpoint only to its own read-back. Under the comparison place “Flag a mismatch for the collections manager before the run reopens.” Add the scope line “The check covers the two ends of the run. The order of the books between them is unverified.” At narrow widths, stack the two stages and repeat the row labels. The public method supplies these relationships. The proposed marks and form arrangement are illustrative. A redacted, publication-cleared route-sheet detail showing both endpoints and the read-back would establish the actual form layout. Neither a destination-page capture nor a successful submission was supplied.
