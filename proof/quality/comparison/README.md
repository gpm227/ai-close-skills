# Two same-input comparisons with public skills

On 23 November 2026, two fresh reviewer sessions independently applied their assigned skill files to the same fictional company and sales snapshots. Each reviewer saw the original fixture request. Each also received a matched request for a partial extraction and pipeline review. Neither received the other reviewer’s instructions, output or expected answers. Both had access to the local source documents. The candidate reviewer had the two department skill directories. The other received local copies of the external publishers’ pinned Markdown instructions, including the brand skill’s named voice companion. This tests how reviewers applied the written instructions on these two cases. The task description explicitly asked for a bridge. No unscripted discovery, installation experience or comparative performance rate was measured.

## Observed results and material differences

| Check | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Extract the published identity and action | Both recovered ink `#142F38`, paper `#FAF6E9`, action `#864018`, the Georgia/Times New Roman heading stack and the Arial/Helvetica body stack. Both identified the live-text header and absence of a logo master. Both used the actual endpoint limitation, current enquiry and subsequent email delivery. Neither disclosed the protected pilot name or number. The CPO run also recovered the source HTML’s 52rem container, 14rem minimum grid tracks and 2rem grid gap. The AI Close run supplied an explicitly proposed matched-endpoint wireframe and identified the particular route-sheet detail required to verify its layout. |
| Reuse voice from two documents | The CPO reviewer gave concrete observations and withheld a full author voice profile because the sample and authorship requirements were unmet. The AI Close reviewer supplied provisional partner guidance, two original before/after rewrites and a proposed email line. The published language supports provisional one-sheet copy. An author-specific profile requires more evidence. Neither obtained owner approval. |
| Reconcile open exposure | Both correctly took four open deals at $230k to three at $200k. B won −60, C lost −40, E newly opened +80, A contracted −30, D expanded +20 (all $k), leaving zero residual. D’s stage move was counted once. Both returned Qualify $80k, Demo $50k and Proposal $70k, one each. A paired-snapshot signed bridge is explicitly required in the AI Close skill. Anthropic’s reviewer calculated it in response to the case request. This case showed no advantage in arithmetic. |
| Diagnose rep exceptions | Both found D’s 50-day activity gap and its explicitly missing next step. Both distinguished the unreported fields for A and E and withheld probabilities and quota coverage. Anthropic specifies cycle-adjusted 14-day screening, stage-median age, repeated slips, contact concentration and two-quarter conversion. AI Close asks for team thresholds or reports the observed age and documents transfer, reopen, unknown-exit and currency accounting. The fixture cannot test historical or CRM-specific capabilities. |

One reproducible check exposes a qualification on the CPO token-audit example. Applying its published example font-family expression to the common source HTML found only the heading declaration. The body sets its family through the CSS `font` shorthand. Inspecting both declaration forms recovered both website stacks. The sample expression missed a font on this fixture. The CPO reviewer recovered both by inspecting the source. A release check that claims complete font verification would need to account for shorthand and the actual output formats.

**Verification and scope.** The source CSS, elapsed days and bridge were checked independently of both reviewers. A literal scan of both outputs confirmed that neither contained the two protected pilot details. No production design, live CTA, contact role, historical conversion, real CRM connector, authenticated ChatGPT or Claude Design installation, native skill routing, external dissemination, or repeat-run reliability was measured.
