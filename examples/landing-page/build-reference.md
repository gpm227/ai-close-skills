# Landing-page build reference

[Empyreal Team](https://empyrealteam.com/) is the reference build for turning an approved brand system into a working lead-generation page. Observed November 23, 2026. Use the live page for the current implementation. Keep its brand, service claims, copy and audience specific to Empyreal.

## What the owner selected as the visual standard

The desktop opening gives roughly half the width to a close-cropped photograph of the freeway interchange. Its concrete supports and traffic put the buyer in a real setting. On the other side, three short lines of sans-serif type lead to one prominent action. Farther down the page, the service choices and custody sequence answer different buyer questions. The same composition with generic copy and a decorative stock image would lose its point.

When comparing a new piece, hide its brand name. Ask a fresh reader what physical job or buyer decision the main image explains and what they can do next. On a stage-repair sheet that means seeing the back of the flat and the precise joint to photograph. On another company's work it needs a different image and arrangement. Keep verified company typography and colors attached to their source and use. Record owner acceptance against the actual render.

## What the partner hands over

The brand system, audience and offer, approved claims and assets, the intended next conversation, service or coverage constraints, and the actual person or system receiving the enquiry.

## What the partner gets

A working responsive page, editable source, the service-to-enquiry path, and evidence that the relevant interactions work. Define the sales handoff before implementation.

| Decision | What the reference does | What to test on a new build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Give a buyer a reason to act | The primary CTA “Make cash earn more” leads to a service conversation. | Read the headline, CTA and form together. The action must deliver the next step promised. |
| Let buyers find their situation | Five service choices have their own discussion links. Coverage and cost comparison offer separate routes. | Start with a specific service, then with a coverage question. Check the actual destination and whether the selected intent survives. |
| Explain the work | The page walks through coordinating pickup, documenting custody and reconciling deposits. | Ask a first-time buyer to explain how the service works and what evidence they will receive. |
| Qualify the enquiry | The form asks for service type, location count and contact details. Consent to respond and optional marketing consent are separate. | Verify labels, validation, optional fields, keyboard/mobile usability and confirmation using an authorized test destination. |
| Carry the lead into sales | The form describes a conversation with the VP of Partnerships. | Trace an authorized test from CTA through received enquiry to the agreed owner, with source and service intact. Measure clicks, received enquiries and qualified meetings separately. |

The reference inspection confirmed the live page, section content, link targets and form fields. Submission, CRM delivery, sales response and conversion performance require separate evidence. The buyer journey is the reusable pattern. Each new company supplies its own voice, decision criteria and destination.
