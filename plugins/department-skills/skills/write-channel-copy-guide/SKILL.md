# Define copy by channel

Give each channel a usable pattern, a real example, and a test that keeps the promise intact as the action changes. For one finished asset, use the corresponding copywriting workflow.

## You'll need

- **Your voice standard.**
- **Claims you can back.**
- **Where each button actually goes.**
- **Approved examples** from each channel.

## Steps

1. Take one real promise and write it for each channel.
2. Homepage: covers the whole business. Detail page: shows inputs and finished work. Email: one decision per email. Buttons: say what clicking does.
3. For each channel, write a before and after and say which words changed, which facts stayed fixed, and why.
4. Keep optional links visually and verbally separate from the main action.

## Check the path before adapting the words

Build a small matrix: placement and variant, buyer question, invariant product fact or qualifier with source ID, what the reader does, what the destination actually does, and the channel's real constraint. Distinguish a supplied screenshot/specification from a live destination you inspected. When there is no working destination, draft a conditional version and hold only the affected action.

Count the final visible words or characters, including spaces and punctuation, in the unit the owner supplied. Never delete the operative word (for example “request”) solely to fit. For subject lines, consider what the recipient will see beside the preheader. For screens, account for disabled, error and success states when the job includes them. Do not turn a confirmation state into a new promise.

## What you get back

A guide with, for each channel: the reader's question, a sensible length, an example, an editing note, and a check that the button matches its destination.

## The trap

The button says "Book a call." The form collects a preferred time and someone confirms by email. That's a request. Say "Request a call."

## Check it

- Every button label matches what happens after the click.
- No channel version adds a benefit the others don't have.

Apply the matrix to [a date request across web, mobile and service email](examples/practice.md).
