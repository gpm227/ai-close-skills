# Practice · Choose the opening for an outbound test

## Run this request

“We book walkthroughs of our maintenance calendar for clinic operations leads. Help me test the introduction. We have 60 eligible clinic groups after exclusions. Each has two contacts. Our reply owner can triage five replies a day. No previous rate is available. We can see assigned accounts, delivery, human replies, booked and held calls. We track replies for 14 calendar days after an account enters.”

Supplied notes: I1, four of five interviews mentioned locating overdue service. I2, one mentioned ease of setup. I3, integrations came up only in our planning meeting. Both versions must offer the same walkthrough. Campaign owner prefers to enroll no more than five new accounts daily. All names and numbers are fictional.

## Worked handoff

**Decision.** Test whether leading with an overdue-service question merits a larger trial. I1 supports overdue work. I2 supports setup as the runner-up. Integrations is an unverified third hypothesis. Compare the selected opening with a neutral feature introduction.

| Decision | A | B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening line | “How do you find overdue service across clinics?” | “We make a maintenance calendar for clinic groups.” |
| Assignment | 30 randomly assigned clinic groups | Remaining 30 clinic groups |
| Other copy, offer and operations | Same sender, subject, walkthrough request, channel, timing and follow-up | Same |

Randomize at account level before Day 0. Both contacts inherit their group's assignment. Stagger balanced A/B entry across workdays with at most five new groups per day. Start with one named contact per account. The second is eligible only if a human handles an explicit referral. Keep that activity in the same arm. If the triage backlog reaches five unresolved replies, pause new enrollment and give the owner the queue.

| When | What the owner does | Exit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry, day 0 | Check current suppression, send the assigned draft after approval | Suppression or undeliverable contact ends automated touches |
| After three days without a reply | Share the same short walkthrough outline with the same CTA | Any human reply exits automation. Route referrals to the owner and honor opt-outs across contacts |
| Day 14 since entry | Close the observation window | Log later replies separately |

**Readout.** Count distinct assigned accounts with at least one explicit request for a walkthrough within 14 days, divided by all assigned accounts in that arm. Record delivery counts separately. An automated reply, referral alone or repeated request adds no qualifying account. Read both arms after the final account’s full window. Track held walkthroughs as a separate downstream count. With no baseline or agreed minimum difference, present counts and uncertainty for the next test. This run cannot establish a winner by itself.

**Checker's example.** A has 30 assigned groups. Two requests came from the same group and a second group asked once. Delivery records show that 28 groups received at least one email. A has 2/30 (6.7%) positive among assigned groups and 2/28 (7.1%) among reached groups. If B has 30 assigned, 30 reached and two positive groups, both B measures are 2/30 (6.7%). The small difference among reached groups disappears on the primary measure.

## Second request

“Halfway through, our rep changed B's offer to a free audit and started contacting both people. Tell me the winning introduction.” A credible read identifies when treatment and contact policy changed, isolates comparable observations if possible, and declines to attribute the mixed-period difference to the introduction.
