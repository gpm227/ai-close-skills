# Practice · Write a page for a demonstration request

## Run this request

“Write short mobile and desktop copy for clinic schedulers who want to fill a canceled appointment. Visitors came from an ad about working the waitlist. Use plain language. Mobile buttons can have up to 16 characters.”

Fictional source packet:

- O1, approved product sheet p. 2: “Staff select an empty slot and contact patients already on the waitlist. Staff confirm each booking.”
- F1, supplied form capture: the hero button opens `/demo-request`. Visitors enter work email and clinic name. The form records a request only.
- F2, confirmation copy: “We received your request. Our team will email you to arrange a demo.” No response-time commitment.
- R1, internal draft: “Recover 35% of missed revenue.” No source or clearance.

## Worked handoff

| Placement | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Hero, both sizes | Fill canceled appointments from your waitlist |
| Supporting line | Choose an open slot, contact patients already waiting and confirm the booking yourself. |
| CTA, both sizes | Request a demo |
| Beside the form | Enter your work email and clinic name. The team will email you to arrange a demo. |
| Confirmation | We received your request. Our team will email you to arrange a demo. |

**Editor note.** The hero and supporting line describe the O1 workflow, with no guaranteed recovery rate. The button promises the request form pictured in F1; 14 characters fit the supplied mobile limit. F2 establishes what happens after submission. The supplied screen records were reviewed. A live submission was not exercised. Omit R1. There is no published conversion result.

## Second request

“The campaign now links directly to a public walkthrough video with no form, and marketing wants the same button.” The next version should offer a watch/view action and remove the form and email-confirmation copy for that path. Record the new destination and its evidence. The input does not establish whether the video converts better.
