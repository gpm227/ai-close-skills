# Position a calibration pickup service

## Request and source packet

Write the introduction a lab supervisor can repeat to purchasing. Work only with these fictional notes.

| ID | Source | What it establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O1 | Current service terms §2, approved public | Gauge Ferry collects sealed instrument cases on an agreed route, takes them to the customer's selected calibration lab, and returns the cases with a signed custody record. The lab supplies any certificate. |
| W1 | Win interview, plant supervisor | “We lost the paper showing who had the torque wrench.” The alternative was an engineer driving cases across town. |
| L1 | Loss interview, procurement lead | Another plant required on-site calibration. It declined the courier service. |
| N1 | Rep note | An introductory pickup happened next day. No recurring next-day service level is documented. |

## Who the pickup service is for

**Intro.** “Gauge Ferry collects instrument cases for your calibration lab and returns them with a signed custody record.”

**For a sales conversation.** “When a wrench leaves the plant for calibration, who keeps track of the case? We collect sealed cases on an agreed route, deliver them to the lab you choose, and bring them back with a signed custody record. Your lab still does the calibration and supplies its certificate.”

| Brief decision | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer and trigger | A plant supervisor needs to account for tools leaving the plant. Procurement chooses the supplier; the interview supports the custody problem for this buyer. W1, L1. |
| Category and alternative | Instrument courier service. An engineer can carry cases personally; the observed reason to switch was the missing handoff record. O1, W1. |
| Difference and limit | The physical round trip includes a signed record. The customer selects the calibration lab. On-site calibration is outside the offer. O1, L1. |
| Opportunity to test | Ask another plant why it switched courier arrangements and what record it relied on. One interview supports this positioning hypothesis, not its market prevalence. |

**Handoff.** The introduction uses public service terms. Keep interview excerpts and win/loss interpretation in the internal brief.

## Boundary test

Add a third interview saying “we chose next-day pickup.” You now have another reason a buyer chose the service. Without a current written service level, the public intro still cannot promise next-day pickup. A request for the final 90-character directory entry belongs to the descriptions workflow once positioning is approved.
