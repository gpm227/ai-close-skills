# Choose a pilot market for dispatch reconciliation

The source cards describe a fictional launch. Run the request with the input before opening the decision key.

## Input

- M1, service owner. For a six-week pilot the team can support one weekly dispatch file in CSV. Native scheduling-system integrations are outside scope. The stated priority is starting the first paid pilot within 30 days.
- M2, district bakeries. Four interviews. Two operators showed weekly CSVs, and one finance director agreed to decide on a paid pilot within 21 days. Both operators currently reconcile missed deliveries on Mondays.
- M3, parcel depots. Nine interviews. Six managers reported more missed scans than the bakeries. All six need an integration before a trial. Budget holder and trial timing unknown.
- M4, catering groups. Two referrals from a supplier. No direct interviews, dispatch sample or known buyer.

**Request.** Choose one segment for this pilot. Explain who has to do what and apply the entry rule to a four-site bakery that has no confirmed CSV.

## Decision key

Recommend district bakery operators **with an obtainable weekly dispatch CSV and a path to an approval decision this month**. M1 is a delivery constraint. M2 supplies evidence for feasibility, the 30-day priority and one buyer's roles. An operator provides the file. Only one observed finance director agreed to decide. Scope that observation to its account. Four sites by itself proves nothing.

| Segment | Disposition | What would change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bakeries | Pursue the observed candidate and qualify similar accounts | No usable export or owner available for the targeted start |
| Depots | Defer the integration-dependent prospects | A prospect agrees to a CSV pilot and identifies its sponsor |
| Catering | Research | Obtain a dispatch sample and an interview with the approval owner |

The four-site bakery goes to research. Ask for a sample export and who could authorize the pilot. Score the decision on evidence, dates and reversibility, not industry headcount.

## Edge prompt

Change M1 so an integration is available, and make the bakery approver unavailable until next quarter. No depot approver has been identified. Show what is newly feasible and the evidence needed before declaring a 30-day winner.
