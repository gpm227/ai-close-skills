# Write company and product descriptions

## Start with

Bring the positioning brief, approved wording, claim register, placement and next action. If evidence is absent, keep the draft to what the company demonstrably delivers.

## Procedure

Write one sentence answering what someone gets. Expand it into a short company description, then a product introduction suited to the placement. Give each sentence a job. Attribute actions to the actual actor. Remove interchangeable adjectives and quantify only supported outcomes. Preserve naming and product boundaries across variants.

## Deliver

Return paste-ready versions labeled by placement. After the copy, map material claims to source IDs and list unresolved publication decisions.

## Check the result

Cover the company name and read the first line. ‘Unlock efficiency’ gives the reader no deliverable. A description naming a workflow, who maintains it and where it runs can be checked against the offer.
