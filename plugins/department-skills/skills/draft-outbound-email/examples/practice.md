# Practice · Email a lab operations lead

## Run this request

“Draft three emails to Jo Ives, operations director at Mesa Lab. Ask whether I can send our sample handoff checklist. We are not sending now.” Everything below is fictional.

| Source | Supplied evidence |
| --- | --- |
| R1, Mesa careers bulletin, 7 April | “Recruiting a second-shift supervisor for sample receiving.” |
| P1, approved sample, p. 1 | Blank fields for outgoing owner, incoming owner and open exceptions |
| P2, guide, p. 2, cleared for outreach | Shows how to mark an exception open and carry it forward unsigned |
| P3, client interview, private | One site said sign-off was faster. No permission to quote and no timing study |

## Emails to the lab operations lead

**Touch 1 · Day 0**

Subject: Second-shift handoffs

Jo, Mesa's posting for a second-shift receiving supervisor caught my eye. We have a blank handoff checklist with room for the outgoing owner, incoming owner and open exceptions. Would you like a copy?

Purpose: Connect the documented hiring event with the artifact. Ask: permission to send the checklist.

**Touch 2 · Day 4 if unanswered**

Subject: One open exception

Jo, one detail in our receiving checklist is a way to carry an exception into the next shift without signing it off as finished. Would the sample be useful?

Purpose: Explain an actual design choice. Ask: permission to send the sample.

**Touch 3 · Day 9 if unanswered**

Subject: Closing the loop

Jo, I’ll leave this here. If the two handoff owners are the part you’re working on, I can send the blank checklist as a starting point. Otherwise I’ll close this out.

Purpose: Give the recipient a low-pressure final opportunity tied to the owner fields. Ask: indicate interest in the sample. The sender initiates no further touch without a reply.

**Evidence and release.** Recipient and title from the brief. Touch 1 cites R1 and P1. Touch 2 uses P2. Touch 3 uses P1. Days 4 and 9 are proposed. No customer result, claim about Mesa's current process or private interview appears. No touch requests a meeting in addition to the sample reply.

Return a labeled outline until those gaps close.
