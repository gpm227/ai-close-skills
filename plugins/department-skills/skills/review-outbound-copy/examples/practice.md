# Practice · Review a claim with protected wording

## Run this request

“For a depot manager. The ask is permission to send the worksheet. Review, keep the subject, and leave the approved sentence exactly as written. Nothing should be sent.” Synthetic approval A1 permits this exact claim in prospect email: “In a four-week pilot at one depot, staff logged 18 handoffs with the worksheet.” A2 permits sharing the blank worksheet. There is no measured time saving.

**Subject:** Shift worksheet

“In a four-week pilot at one depot, staff logged 18 handoffs with the worksheet. It saves hours every week. Can I send the blank worksheet, or would Tuesday work for a demo?”

## The revised email

**Subject:** Shift worksheet

In a four-week pilot at one depot, staff logged 18 handoffs with the worksheet. Can I send the blank worksheet?

| Original passage | Disposition | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “In a four-week pilot … with the worksheet.” | Keep whole sentence verbatim. | A1 covers wording, duration, count, location and channel. |
| “It saves hours every week.” | Remove. | A1 measures use only. A time-saving claim would need a timing study and clearance. |
| “, or would Tuesday work for a demo” | Remove second ask. | Owner requested permission to send the worksheet. A2 allows it. |

Ready for owner review. The original subject and protected sentence survive exactly. A single reply can now complete the intended action. The clean version is shorter.
