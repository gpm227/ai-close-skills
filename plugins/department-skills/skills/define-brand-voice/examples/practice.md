## The editor’s rule

| Scope | Rule, exception and evidence | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer service updates; supported pattern | Give the verified person or team, the physical check, and the event that triggers contact. If the technician is unknown use the real team. An estimated finish date needs separate evidence. E1, E2. | “Your job is pending.” → “Our bench team is checking the catch. We’ll email after the test.” Use this example only for a ticket with that check and contact method. |
| Greeting in welcome email; provisional | E3 supports first-name address in that particular relationship. Check other approved welcomes before making it a rule. | Preserve an approved formal greeting on a first-contact letter. |

**Held-out rewrite of T1.** “Omar is checking the latch. He’ll email after the test.” The agent, component and trigger all match the ticket. Publication clearance for personal names or customer examples remains a separate decision. A reusable external guide can substitute a fictional name or team and regenerate the example.

## Boundary test

A service lawyer requires the exact capitalized phrase “Inspection in Progress” on the warranty portal. Keep it intact there. A copied occurrence of E1 in a newsletter is still one editorial decision. Neither piece changes the evidence count for the service-update rule.
