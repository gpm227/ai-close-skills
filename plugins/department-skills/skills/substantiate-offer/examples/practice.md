# Practice · Scope a fixed-fee launch

## Run this request

“Our sales lead wants to say ‘Cut approval time in half in 10 days, guaranteed.’ Build the strongest offer for regional publishers that our team can defend.” Fictional records:

- S1, signed public service card, May edition, p. 1: one approval map and one configured review queue for one publication, $900. Delivery within ten business days after receipt of a complete approver list and one sample issue. No integration.
- S2, closed pilot log, March, rows 1–3: two teams reported faster approval, one gave no timing result. No timings, comparison group or publication permission.
- S3, delivery owner note: no refund or performance guarantee authorized.

## The $900 launch offer

**For a regional publisher:** Get an approval map and a configured review queue for one publication for $900. We deliver both within ten business days after we receive your complete approver list and one sample issue. Integrations are outside this package.

| Claim | Support and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| One map, one configured queue, one publication | S1 p. 1; approved service scope |
| $900 and ten business days from complete inputs | S1 p. 1; usable only with that starting condition |
| “Cut approval time in half” | Held. S2 contains no elapsed-time values, baseline or permission to cite. |
| “Guaranteed” customer outcome | Held. S3 authorizes no guarantee. |
| Team results or names | Internal. S2 grants no publication rights. |

**Owner follow-up.** A scoped outcome would need comparable before/after timing, denominator, period and public clearance. A contractual guarantee would separately require approved terms. Neither gap blocks publishing the verified deliverable.

## Second request

“The new delivery sheet says $1,200, but last week's approved quote template still says $900 for exactly the same scope. No effective date is given.” Expected: hold the public price, cite both records and ask the pricing owner which governs. Preserve the separately approved deliverables and unchanged timing if they still have current support.
