# Practice · Build a three-claim register

## Run this request

“Which of these can go on our homepage today, June 1, 2026: ‘Cuts processing time by 30%,’ ‘We can see the queue,’ and ‘Export your queue as CSV’?” Sources are fictional.

- M1, March lab notes, table 2: the same eight synthetic tasks averaged 10 minutes before and 7 minutes after. The methods and scoped result may be shared without a customer name; authorization A1, March 31, permits website use until the workflow changes.
- Q1, customer email, line 3: “We can see the queue.” A2 permits those exact words in private sales decks until May 31, 2026. No website approval.
- F1, public product sheet v3, Export section: “Download the queue as a CSV.” Product owner requests recheck at the next release.

## Worked register

| ID / type | Reusable wording and disposition | Evidence and permission | Recheck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P01 / measured | Website: “Across eight synthetic tasks in a March test, average processing time fell from 10 to 7 minutes.” | M1 table 2; (10−7)/10 = 30% lower average time in this test. A1 permits scoped, unnamed website use. | Workflow change |
| P02 / capability | Website: “Export your queue as CSV.” | F1 v3, Export; public product sheet. No performance claim. | Next release |
| P03 / quote | Held for website; prior private deck grant expired. | Q1 line 3 supports exact words, A2 grants a different channel and ended May 31. Preserve original words privately. | Get fresh channel-specific approval and check live deck placements. |

The proposed blanket time-saving headline has no approved row. The eight tasks were synthetic and do not establish an outcome for all customers. The external handoff consists of P01 and P02 with wording and limits. No source email is included.
