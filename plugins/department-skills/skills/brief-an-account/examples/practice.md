# A rep’s pre-outreach brief

The case is fictional. Use only these dated source excerpts. Today's date is 6 March 2026.

## Input

- P1, buyer press release, 12 February: “Rill Cold Storage will open a second cross-dock in Ames this summer, subject to permitting.”
- J1, careers listing, 1 March: “Two dispatcher openings, Cedar Falls night shift.” No reason for hiring is stated.
- C1, CRM note from 4 March: “Our rep thinks the Ames site is already operating.” No buyer statement.
- V1, product sheet: Our software reconciles daily dock and dispatch logs. No evidence identifies the current vendor.
- Recipient: Morgan, operations manager. First outreach.

**Request.** Give the rep a one-page brief, two testable hypotheses, and one opening question.

## Decision key

Lead with the planned, permit-dependent Ames opening (P1) and the two Cedar Falls roles (J1). Include both source dates. A March CRM note written later by a rep does not establish that the opening happened. Cedar Falls and Ames are different locations. Record the current log system as unknown.

| Hypothesis | Evidence | What could rule it out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planning the second site may require a new handoff between sites | P1, pending opening; V1 for the possible fit | The workflows will operate independently, or permitting delays the opening |
| Night-shift coverage may change the dispatch-to-dock handoff at Cedar Falls | J1 | The hiring replaces departing dispatchers without changing the process |

Accept an open question such as “How do dispatch and dock teams share the end-of-shift log today?” Reject “How much time is the new site already costing you?” The latter asserts both the opening and the pain.

## Edge prompt

A new dated notice P2 says the permit was denied and the project suspended. Revise the brief, remove or update the Ames hypothesis, preserve the Cedar Falls fact and explain why today's question changes or still works.
