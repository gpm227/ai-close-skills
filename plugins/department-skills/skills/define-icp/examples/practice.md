# Qualification card for a cold-chain audit service

All records are fictional. In a fresh conversation, paste only the input and request. Compare the answer with the decision key afterward.

## Input

- O1, service director’s signed service sheet, 3 April. “We audit chilled food depots in Iowa. The contracting entity must operate 2–6 chilled depots in Iowa, inclusive. Existing customers go to the account team. Dry warehouses and franchisees owned by someone else do not count.”
- W1, closed-won interview. A new compliance lead called after a failed inspection. Two depots needed a repeatable receiving check.
- L1, closed-lost note. Another prospect had a new compliance lead and stayed with its internal team. No reason given.
- A, verified directory plus customer register. Kettle operates two chilled Iowa depots, one Nebraska site and four dry Iowa warehouses. No customer match.
- B, verified directory. Mallow lists five Iowa depots. Two belong to a separately owned franchise and the other three have no temperature classification. Customer register unavailable.
- C, customer register. Reed is an active customer. Its site roster is missing.

**Request.** Draft a qualification card that two reps can use. Apply it to A, B and C. Explain what the wins and losses can support.

## Decision key

| ID | Decision | Evidence and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Eligible | Two qualifying depots meet the inclusive floor in O1. The other five sites add zero to the count. The register clears the customer exclusion. |
| B | Needs evidence | Verify which of the three owned depots are chilled and check the customer register. The two franchise sites do not count. |
| C | Excluded from acquisition | The register confirms the routing rule in O1 even with no site count. Route to the account team. |

The card identifies the contracting entity, unit, geography, endpoints, source of each rule and exclusion precedence. A new hire is a hypothesis from W1/L1. No rule or probability follows from the hire or the lost deal. A failed inspection is an observed trigger in W1.

## Edge prompt

Replace O1 with two equally current, unsigned sales notes, one applying the band to national sites and the other to Iowa sites. A prospect has four in Iowa and eight nationally. Show both outcomes and the single owner decision that settles classification. Do not promote either definition to a hard rule.
