# Compare two support-desk proposals

**Request.** “We need support for 500 cases each month for a year. Single sign-on is mandatory. Compare pre-tax USD cash and commitments. All amounts below are USD.”

**Quote A §2:** $400/month including 300 cases/month; $2 for each additional case in that month. No setup fee; billed monthly in arrears at month-end. Month-to-month, cancellable before a new month begins. **A §4:** SSO included. **Quote B §1:** $8,400 non-cancellable, prepaid for the year; covers up to 600 cases in each calendar month. **B §3:** additional cases above 600 cost $4 each; no setup fee. B does not address SSO. Both quotes include support; compare 12 complete months beginning on the first.

## Worked decision

| Item | A | B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At 500 cases/month | 12 × (400 + 200 × 2) = $9,600 (A §2) | $8,400 prepayment (B §1) |
| Cash timing | $800 at each month-end | $8,400 upfront |
| Initial binding term | One service month, $400 minimum before usage | Twelve months, $8,400 before overage |
| SSO | Met (A §4) | Unconfirmed; no SSO clause |

A meets the requirement today. B offers $1,200 less scheduled annual cash at the given volume *if* SSO is included, with the full year due upfront. Procurement asks B, “Is SSO included at this price throughout the term? Please identify the contract clause and any mandatory fee.” A price ranking alone cannot qualify B.

Within 300–600 steady cases/month, solve 12 × [400 + 2 × (v − 300)] = 8,400: tie at 450 cases/month. At 400, A costs $7,200; at 500, A costs $9,600. Beyond 600 cases/month the B formula changes and the prior tie calculation cannot be extrapolated.

## Different case

“SSO is now explicitly included. Our 6,000 cases arrive as 1,000/month for six months and zero for six; both vendors remain subscribed all year.” A: 6 × $1,800 + 6 × $400 = $13,200. B: $8,400 + 6 × 400 × $4 = $18,000. A is cheaper by $4,800. A model that averages this to 500/month picks the wrong price leader. If the buyer plans to cancel A in idle months, ask about the actual calendar and restart conditions before changing the result.
