# Draft LinkedIn outreach

## Inputs

Relevant public profile excerpt with date, relationship stage, buyer problem and offer. Accept pasted material or files. Use the buyer’s definitions and label gaps that affect the result.

## Do the work

Choose one relevant professional detail verified in the supplied material. Write for the actual connection state. Distinguish an invitation from a post-acceptance note; give a second message a separate reason to exist. Stop after a refusal.

## Return

Connection-context note and two short post-acceptance drafts. Give the complete free result in the current workspace. Draft external actions for the user to review.

## Check before delivery

A repost is evidence of sharing a topic, not evidence the person authored it or owns the budget. Apply any additional acceptance conditions supplied by the team lead. Identify which checks passed and what remains unresolved.
