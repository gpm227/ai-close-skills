---
name: write-outbound-email
description: Draft an outbound email and follow-ups for a defined buyer using the team's ICP, offer, proof, and voice. Use when a team needs reviewable email copy for a sequence.
---

Read the buyer or ICP, offer, available evidence, desired reply, and requested number of touches. Reuse supplied brand voice and approved copy examples. If the audience, offer, or deliverable next step is unclear, ask one focused question. If voice guidance is missing, use plain professional language and label that assumption in the handoff.

1. Choose one buyer problem supported by the brief. Phrase an unverified problem as a possibility. Only use personalization, numerical claims, or customer results supported by the source material.
2. Draft the requested number of touches. Default to an opener and one follow-up when unspecified. For each follow-up, introduce a concrete detail, use case, or piece of proof from the brief. Keep the same next step unless the brief explicitly changes it.
3. Apply the team's confirmed standard. As a starter, use one reply-sized decision per email. Count commitments across the entire body. A request for a document and an invitation to meet are two decisions even in a single sentence. Check that the sender can deliver what the reader is invited to request.
4. Return a subject and body for each touch. Keep assumptions, evidence notes, and unresolved details outside the buyer-facing copy. Produce drafts for the team to review.

For a synthetic practice brief and examples of how to check the output, read [the worksheet sequence](references/worksheet-sequence.md).
