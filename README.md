## Try it on your work

Download the ZIP for your job. In a ChatGPT workspace with Skills enabled, open **Plugins → Skills → Create → Upload from your computer** and select the ZIP. Start a new chat, name the skill, and attach the material you already work from. Each ZIP includes a worked example. [Manual setup and installation help](company/DEPLOY.md#run-one-job-in-chatgpt)

For example, attach your open-deal export and use this request.

> Use the pipeline review skill to prepare Monday’s meeting. Which deals need a decision? Show the deal, the reason it needs attention, and what the owner should do next.

## Make a skill your team’s own

Say your CRM export has one row per contact. A deal with three contacts can show up three times. Your team can keep that export, the correct deal total, and the counting rule together in its private repository. When someone catches another mistake, the **Improve a skill** workflow uses their input and the expected result to propose a change your team can test, approve, and share.
